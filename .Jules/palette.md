
## 2026-05-31 - Completeness in Micro-UX Mockups
**Learning:** When implementing interactive mockups (like the USSD simulator), partial localization (e.g., only the header) can feel "half-baked". A complete micro-UX improvement should ensure all visible state-dependent content is updated to provide a cohesive experience.
**Action:** When adding state-driven content updates, trace all visible elements that should logically change with that state and ensure they are all included in the implementation.
