## 2025-07-31 - [Interactive Multilingual Simulators & Technical Feedback Loops]
**Learning:** For mockups simulating physical/technical systems (like USSD or hardware terminals), static mockups offer low perceived value. Making the mockup interactive and pairing it with a real-time reactive log console utilizing ARIA `role="log"` and `aria-live="polite"` drastically increases user comprehension and screen-reader accessibility. Storing log histories, capping them cleanly (e.g., `.slice(-10)`), and adding simple text downloads/resets adds premium UX delight with minimal complexity.
**Action:** Always map simulator language choices and option selections to centralized reactive state. Ensure all mockup action buttons are standard semantic `button` tags with proper `aria-pressed` states, focus-visible indicators, and screen-reader polite log announcements to maintain excellent web accessibility.
