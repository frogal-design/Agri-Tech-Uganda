## 2026-07-05 - Interactive USSD Simulator Feedback
**Learning:** Synchronizing a visual mockup (USSD phone) with a secondary feedback loop (Debug Console) significantly enhances the "perceived utility" and "delight" of a simulation-based UI.
**Action:** In simulators or complex interactive components, look for opportunities to provide immediate, multi-layered feedback (visual + technical logs) for user actions.
