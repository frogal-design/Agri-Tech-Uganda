## 2025-05-14 - [Technical Simulator Localization Accuracy]
**Learning:** Accurate localized content in technical mockups (like USSD simulators) significantly increases 'perceived utility' and user trust. Hallucinated or incorrect translations can break the immersion and reduce the effectiveness of the accessibility demonstration.
**Action:** Always cross-reference localized strings with existing patterns in the codebase or request verification for regional dialects to ensure cultural and technical accuracy.
