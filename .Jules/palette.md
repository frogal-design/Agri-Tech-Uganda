## 2025-05-14 - Interactive Language Selection in USSD Simulator
**Learning:** Converting static mockups into interactive elements with state-based localized feedback significantly enhances the "lived-in" feel of a micro-UI without requiring a full backend. Semantic buttons with `aria-pressed` provide a robust foundation for both accessibility and visual state.
**Action:** Use `aria-pressed` on semantic `<button>` elements for selection toggles and synchronize mockup content with local state to provide immediate, localized feedback.
