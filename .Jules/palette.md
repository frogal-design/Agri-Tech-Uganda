## 2025-05-14 - Localized Interactive Micro-UX
**Learning:** In low-connectivity simulator environments (like USSD), providing immediate localized visual feedback through semantic interactive elements (buttons instead of divs) significantly improves both accessibility and the perceived "live" quality of the simulation.
**Action:** Always prefer semantic <button> elements for selection states and synchronize secondary UI elements (like terminal headers) with user-controlled state to provide delightful, multi-point feedback.
